About विशालवृत्तावलिः (Viśālavṛttāvaliḥ)

विशालवृत्तावलिः (Viśālavṛttāvaliḥ) is a comprehensive Sanskrit prosody and poetic composition suite designed and maintained by Balaji Baskaran.

Sanskrit prosody or Chandaśāstra (छन्दशास्त्र) is the classical science of poetic meters and verse structures in Sanskrit. Every Sanskrit meter (chanda) carries a distinct cadence, emotional aesthetic (rasa), and rhythmic flow.

Meter Classifications:
  • Akṣaragaṇa-vṛtta (Syllabic Meters):
    • Sama-vṛtta: All 4 quarters (pādas) share identical gaṇa patterns (e.g., Anuṣṭubh, Indravajrā, Vasantatilakā, Śārdūlavikrīḍita, Mandākrāntā, Śikhariṇī).
    • Ardhasama-vṛtta: Alternating patterns between odd (1, 3) and even (2, 4) quarters (e.g., Viyoginī, Puṣpitāgrā).
    • Viṣama-vṛtta: Heterogeneous meters where all 4 quarters differ.
    • Upajāti (उपजाति): Hybrid verses combining related meters (such as 16 classical forms of Indravajrā + Upendravajrā, or Vaṃśastha + Indravaṃśā).
  • Mātrā-vṛtta (Moraic Meters): Measured by temporal morae (mātrās) per quarter rather than fixed syllables (e.g., Āryā, Gīti, Upagīti, Udgīti).
