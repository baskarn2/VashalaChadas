About Sanskrit Prosody (Chandaśāstra)

Sanskrit prosody or chandaśāstra (छन्दशास्त्र) is the ancient traditional science of poetic meters and verse structures in Sanskrit literature.

Every Sanskrit meter (chanda) carries its own distinct rhythm, cadence, and aesthetic movement. Sanskrit meters are broadly categorized into:

  • Akṣaragaṇa-vṛtta (Vṛtta): Syllabic meters based on fixed combinations of light (laghu) and heavy (guru) syllables arranged into 8 standard triads called gaṇas.
    • Sama-vṛtta: All 4 quarters (pādas) have identical rhythmic patterns (e.g., Anuṣṭubh, Indravajrā, Vasantatilakā, Śārdūlavikrīḍita, Mandākrāntā).
    • Ardhasama-vṛtta: Odd quarters (1 & 3) follow one pattern, and even quarters (2 & 4) follow another (e.g., Viyoginī, Puṣpitāgrā).
    • Viṣama-vṛtta: All 4 quarters have differing structures.
    • Upajāti: Hybrid verses combining related meters (such as Indravajrā and Upendravajrā).
  • Mātrā-vṛtta (Jāti): Moraic meters defined by counting total temporal units (mātrās) per quarter rather than fixed individual syllables (e.g., Āryā, Gīti, Upagīti).

About Chandojñānam Local Application

Chandojñānam (छन्दोज्ञानम्) is a comprehensive meter identification and scansion system with a database of over 200+ Sanskrit meters collated from classic authoritative sources including Vṛttaratnākara (वृत्तरत्नाकर) and Śabdakalpadruma (शब्दकल्पद्रुम).

Key capabilities:

Multi-Script Input & Output

Supports Devanagari, IAST, ITRANS, Harvard-Kyoto, SLP1, WX, Bengali, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Tamil, Oriya, and more.

Smart Fuzzy Matching

Ranks closest meters using Levenshtein distance on syllable weight vectors and produces exact edit suggestions for poetic corrections.

Verse & Line Modes

Analyze verses as 4-pada compositions with overall meter inference, or analyze individual isolated lines.

Export & Statistics

Download full scansion results in JSON or TXT formats and review aggregated meter distribution statistics.
